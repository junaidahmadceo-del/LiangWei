Written for people who take pills

The part most remedy books leave out.

Herbs are real medicine. That is why they help, and it is why they can mix badly with the pills you already take. Here are a few of the warnings inside:

Ginger

If you take a blood thinner

Food and tea amounts are fine for most people. Large amounts or pills can add to the thinning.

Licorice root

If you have high blood pressure

It can raise blood pressure. The book tells you the safer form to use.

Hibiscus tea

If you take blood pressure pills

It lowers blood pressure too, so it can add to your pills. Check your readings.

Goji berries

If you take warfarin

They can make warfarin stronger. Ask your doctor before eating them often.

Grapefruit

If you take a statin

It can raise the level of some statins in your blood. Ask your pharmacist.

Psyllium and flaxseed

With any daily pill

They can stop your pills from being absorbed. Take them two hours apart.

Grandmother's Steamed Pear

A classic home remedy for a dry cough and a sore, dry throat. Any firm pear will do.

  1. Cut the top off the pear and keep it as a lid. Scoop out the core.
  2. Put a spoon of sugar or honey inside and put the lid back on.
  3. Stand it in a bowl in a covered pot with 2 inches of water. Steam on low for 45 to 60 minutes.
Before you try thisIf you have diabetes, leave out the sugar. A cough lasting more than three weeks, or with blood, fever or breathlessness, needs a doctor.
